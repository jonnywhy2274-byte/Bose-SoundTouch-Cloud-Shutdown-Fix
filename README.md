# Bose SoundTouch Cloud Shutdown Fix: Controller Pro, Controller Cube & Controller Cloud

On May 6, 2026, Bose permanently shut down the cloud servers powering the SoundTouch ecosystem. This repository serves as the official documentation and resource hub for the **Controller Pro** ecosystem—a complete local software, turnkey hardware, and cloud suite designed to permanently rescue orphaned Bose SoundTouch speakers.

Whether you just want a fast local Wi-Fi remote, a 100% private local server with zero ongoing fees, or an instant cloud-hosted bridge, the ecosystem is structured into three clear tiers:

### 2. 🧊 Controller Cube (Turnkey Hardware & DIY Master OS)
Our flagship 100% local, zero-subscription Bose cloud replacement. Running silently on your home network, the **Controller Cube** emulates the retired `marge` and `bmx` servers locally to permanently restore:
- **Physical Presets 1–6:** Burns TuneIn stations, custom streams, and NAS folders directly into speaker NVRAM for phone-free one-touch playback.
- **Native Direct Streaming & HTTPS Audio Proxy:** Restores native TuneIn radio and custom raw stream URLs with on-the-fly SSL decryption.
- **Smart NAS Injector & Native Stereo Pairing:** Pair brand-new UPnP/DLNA NAS drives (`STORED_MUSIC`) and bond Left/Right stereo pairs (`ST-10`, etc.) over the air.

## ⚡ 100% Wireless Zero-Touch Speaker Adoption (v9.0+)
USB thumb drives and manual SSH/Telnet terminal commands are officially retired. 
1. Plug your **Controller Cube** into your router (or boot **Controller Cube OS**).
2. Open the **Controller Pro** app on your phone or tablet.
3. Tap the **Speaker Icon (top-left) > Network & Speakers**, and turn ON **Auto-Link Speakers**.
4. The app automatically discovers your unadopted SoundTouch speakers and links them over your Wi-Fi in seconds.
